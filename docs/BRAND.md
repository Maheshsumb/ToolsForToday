Tokens live in src/app/globals.css. Poppins 600 is used for headings; Inter 400 for body text. Fontsource packages bundle fonts locally with swap and system fallbacks; no runtime Google Fonts requests are required. Add other weights only when used.

Use navy text on white or mist backgrounds. Brand accents on this initial page are decorative. Do not assume white text on orange, yellow, or other light brand colours is accessible. Verify text and focus contrast for every future pairing.

The existing logo has an orange curly/script For between bold navy Tools and Today. Do not recreate or alter it. Approved assets belong in public/brand/. The owner will supply approved assets separately. Parent workspace images remain unchanged; the confirmation page uses plain text.
